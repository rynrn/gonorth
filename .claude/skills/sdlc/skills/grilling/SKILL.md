# Requirements interview

Interview the user about the requested feature until the consequential decision branches are settled. Inspect available code and facts yourself. Ask one question at a time, include a recommended answer and explain its trade-off. Resolve dependencies before dependent decisions. The user owns decisions; do not act on the design until confirmed. Finish with a short list of agreed requirements and assumptions for plan-loop. Stay within the requested scope.
