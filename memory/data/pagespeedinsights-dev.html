What is PageSpeed Insights?

PageSpeed Insights is your gateway to understanding website performance. Our Speedtest Google tool measures key metrics like loading speed (LCP), responsiveness (INP), and visual stability (CLS) – the same metrics Google uses to rank websites.

Running a Google Speedtest shows you exactly how search engines see your site. Faster websites rank higher and provide visitors with a noticeably better experience.

Our PageSpeed Insights analyzer breaks down each metric and shows you exactly what's slowing things down.

Why Run a Speedtest Google Check?

Nobody likes waiting. Research shows that over half of mobile visitors will leave if a page takes more than 3 seconds to load. A quick Google Speedtest reveals if your site is driving customers away.

Every extra second of loading time can hurt your conversions significantly. With our free PageSpeed Insights tool, you'll see exactly how your site performs – using real Google data.

Run your Speedtest Google analysis on both mobile and desktop to get personalized recommendations for making your site faster.
